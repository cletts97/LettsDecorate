## New writing and design

Headlines, short service descriptions and calls to action are newly written draft marketing copy, based on the general services evidenced by the old site. They are not represented as quotes from the owner. Please obtain his approval.

The proposed palette is off-white, deep green/charcoal, muted clay and sage. It is independent of paint manufacturers and does not promise an on-screen colour match.

The main hero and the placeholder gallery panels are abstract CSS graphics. They are not photographs and are not presented as completed customer projects. Placeholder cards are explicitly labelled "Photographs coming soon".

The gallery accepts local image files when they become available. The homepage shows the first three valid entries. It does not upload images to Azure, store them in a database or ask customers to submit images through the website.

## Scope decisions

- Existing public page addresses are kept; Services and a website-information page are added.
- No WordPress/PHP/database dependencies or third-party analytics.
- No stock-photo claims, invented customer names, opening hours, physical street address, price list, availability guarantee or free-quotation promise.
- Customer review snippets have been deliberately kept short. The old review-submission form is replaced with an email feedback link.
- No full legal/compliance assessment has been performed. The technical privacy information needs an owner review before launch.
- It is a static site, not a CMS. Changes are made through files and published through GitHub/Azure.
