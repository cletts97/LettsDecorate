THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

More than
a coat of paint.

Scott established Letts Decorate in 2015, naming the company in memory of his late father, Garry Letts.

After studying painting and decorating at Folkestone College between 2008 and 2010, he built hands-on experience working for other companies before starting his own.

That personal connection shapes the business: taking pride in the work, treating customers with care, and helping people feel at ease with their choice of style and colour.

Based in Ashford and working across Kent, Letts Decorate has grown through customers recommending the business to friends and family.
